Free Dental Chart Generator

Create a free dental chart online with a responsive odontogram tool for exams, reports, education, and treatment planning. The generator supports FDI, Universal, and Palmer tooth notation, full-mouth charts, upper arch views, lower arch views, square printable layouts, tooth condition templates, and selected-tooth export data.

Use the interactive dental chart to mark caries, fillings, watch areas, periodontal notes, surgery plans, and other visual findings. Charts can be exported as SVG for editable vector graphics, PNG for documents and presentations, or JSON for structured selected-teeth data and chart settings.
